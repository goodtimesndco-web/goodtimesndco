FAQ

Common Questions

How far in advance should we book?

Most clients book 3–9 months out, especially for Saturday weddings during peak season (May–October). Popular dates can sell out even earlier, so we recommend checking availability as soon as your venue is confirmed.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes — a 50% deposit is required to confirm your date, with the remaining balance due before your event. A booking through our online system reserves your date and puts you in "pending" status; our team follows up within 1 business day to confirm details and collect the deposit, which moves you to "confirmed."

How much space does the booth need?

Our open-air setup needs roughly a 10' x 10' footprint plus access to a standard power outlet. We'll confirm exact space and load-in details with your venue ahead of time.

What's included in the digital gallery?

Every photo taken during your session, delivered as a private online gallery within 48 hours (same-day for the Essential package, same-night for the Bespoke package), ready to download and share.

Do you travel outside your standard service area?

Yes — reach out via the form above with your venue location and we'll include any travel fee in your quote for venues outside our standard radius.

Do you offer corporate or non-wedding pricing?

Corporate events, brand activations, and other non-wedding bookings are priced from our separate corporate rate card. Select your event type on the booking form and your estimate will update automatically.
