Our Story

Good Times, By Design

We started Good Times & Co. because the photobooths we kept seeing at weddings felt like an afterthought. We build the opposite: a considered, styled experience worthy of the rest of your event.

What We Believe

Details Make the Night

Every backdrop, prop, and print template is chosen to complement your event — never a generic template slapped on a cheap frame. Our attendants arrive early, dress formally, and stay focused on your guests all night.

We limit ourselves to one event per date so every couple and host gets our full attention, from first call to final gallery delivery.
